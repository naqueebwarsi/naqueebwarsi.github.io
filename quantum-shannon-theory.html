Quantum Shannon Theory

Information-theoretic limits of communication and information processing over quantum channels, from one-shot protocols to asymptotic capacity questions.

Research accomplished

Fundamental Limits of Quantum Communication

A substantial part of my work develops one-shot methods for quantum communication. Using hypothesis testing and convex-split techniques, this research establishes achievability bounds and protocols for communication tasks over noisy quantum channels and networks. The work addresses state redistribution, measurement compression, source and message compression, and communication with side information or entanglement assistance.

A related line studies compound and adversarial channel models, including classical–quantum arbitrarily varying multiple-access channels, authenticated communication, and commitment over classical–quantum channels. Across these problems, the common theme is to identify operational limits through non-asymptotic information-theoretic tools.
